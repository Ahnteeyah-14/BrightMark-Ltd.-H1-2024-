# # BrightMark-Ltd.-H1-2024-
Sales Performance Analysis
##  1. Introduction

Data-driven decision-making is essential for organizations seeking to improve performance and maximize profitability. This project simulates a real-world business scenario in which I analyzed BrightMark Ltd.'s H1 2024 sales data using Microsoft Excel and Power Query.

The goal was to transform raw, inconsistent sales data into an interactive executive dashboard that provides meaningful insights into product performance, regional sales, sales representative productivity, pricing strategy, and overall business performance.

This project demonstrates the complete data analysis workflow—from data cleaning and transformation to visualization, interpretation, and strategic business recommendations.

##  2. Problem Statement

BrightMark Ltd.'s management noticed inconsistencies in sales performance during the first half of 2024 and requested an analysis to understand what was driving revenue.

##  The CEO wanted answers to the following business questions:

Which product categories generated the highest revenue?
Which regions performed best and worst?
Are experienced sales representatives outperforming newly hired representatives?
Does increasing discounts actually improve revenue?
How did revenue change from January to June 2024?

However, the available dataset contained several quality issues that prevented accurate reporting.

# 7. Data Analysis
##  KPI Cards
💰 Total Revenue — $46,718,368

This represents the total revenue generated during the first half of 2024.

It serves as the primary indicator of overall business performance.

📦 Total Orders — 289

A total of 289 completed customer orders were recorded.

Tracking order volume helps measure customer activity and sales demand.

🛒 Total Quantity Sold — 2,940 Units

This KPI measures the total number of products sold during H1 2024.

It provides insight into product movement and customer purchasing behavior.

💵 Average Revenue per Order — $15,891

This KPI measures the average value generated from each completed order.

A higher average order value generally indicates stronger sales performance.

##  Revenue by Product Category

Key Insight

Electronics generated the highest revenue ($25.3M).
Furniture followed with $18.3M.
Cleaning Products, Office Supplies, and Stationery collectively contributed less than 7% of total revenue.
Business Insight

Electronics and Furniture are the company's primary revenue drivers. Together, they account for over 93% of total revenue, highlighting both their importance and the company's dependence on these two product categories.

##  Revenue by Region

Key Insight

Enugu generated the highest revenue ($11.6M).
Kano and Abuja followed closely.
Port Harcourt generated the lowest revenue ($5.1M).
Business Insight

The company performs exceptionally well in Enugu, Kano, and Abuja. Port Harcourt presents an opportunity for targeted marketing initiatives and sales improvement strategies.

##  Monthly Revenue Trend

Key Insight

Revenue declined from January through March before recovering strongly in April.

Performance remained relatively stable during May and June.

Business Insight

The decline in Q1 followed by recovery in Q2 suggests possible seasonal demand or the impact of marketing campaigns. Understanding the drivers behind April's rebound could help sustain future growth.

##  Sales Representative Performance

Key Insight

Experienced Representatives: $24.0M
New Representatives: $22.8M
Business Insight

Experienced representatives slightly outperformed newer hires, reflecting the value of experience while also showing that newly recruited staff are already contributing significantly to overall sales.

##  Discount vs Revenue

Key Insight

The scatter plot produced an R² value of 0.6462, indicating that higher discounts did not consistently lead to higher revenue.

Business Insight

This finding suggests that the current discount strategy may not be maximizing profitability. Instead of applying broad discounts, the company should adopt more targeted promotional strategies.

##  8. Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following recommendations are proposed:

1. Optimize the Discount Strategy

Since higher discounts did not consistently generate higher revenue, the company should review its pricing strategy and focus on targeted promotions that improve both sales and profitability.

2. Strengthen Underperforming Product Categories

Electronics and Furniture account for most of the company's revenue. Increasing marketing efforts for Cleaning Products, Office Supplies, and Stationery could diversify revenue streams and reduce dependence on only two categories.

3. Improve Regional Performance

Develop targeted marketing campaigns and customer engagement strategies for Port Harcourt to improve sales performance and expand market share.

4. Continue Investing in Sales Development

Provide ongoing training, mentoring, and performance support for new sales representatives to help them reach the productivity levels of experienced staff more quickly.
